Our Services

ROTOSCOPE

Precise frame-by-frame masking and isolation of objects, characters, and elements for seamless compositing and visual effects integration.

PAINT-PREP

Expert paint and prep work including wire removal, rig removal, object cleanup, and preparing clean plates for seamless compositing.

COSMETIC FIXES

Beauty work, skin retouching, blemish removal, and digital makeup to enhance talent appearance and maintain visual consistency.

MATCHMOVE

Camera tracking and 3D matchmoving to seamlessly integrate CG elements with live-action footage, ensuring perfect perspective and movement.

COMPOSITING

Expert layering and blending of multiple elements including live-action, CG, matte paintings, and effects to create the final seamless shot.

CG

Full 3D computer graphics including modeling, texturing, lighting, rendering, and animation for photorealistic digital assets and environments.
